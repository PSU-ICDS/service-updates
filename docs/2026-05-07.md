#### Incident

 - Date: May 7, 2026
 - CVE: CVE-2026-43284 — Dirty Frag
 - Status: **Resolved**

#### Summary

We were made aware of an exploit that would use specific networking paths to exploit our systems. This exploit makes use of kernel modules that are not normally loaded on our cluster. We disabled these kernel modules quickly and no reboot or workload interruptions were required to mitigate this exploit.
